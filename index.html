As Web3 adoption grows, millions of new users enter crypto with little understanding of blockchain risks — and unlike traditional banking, crypto transactions are often irreversible. A single mistake can mean permanent loss. This tool aims to provide a practical pre-transaction safety assessment to identify risks before funds are transferred.

Future Vision

CERIP explores how risk indicators, open-source information, transaction activity, behavioural patterns, and publicly available intelligence can be structured for earlier identification of potential scams and suspicious activity.
