Attribution Assessment & Limitations

Based on the available on-chain activity, wallet interactions, transaction timing, funding behaviour, and publicly accessible OSINT indicators, several observable connections were identified between the analysed wallets and associated social activity.

The investigation identified behavioural patterns including wallet funding flows, intermediary transfers, token deployment activity, and movement of funds through centralised service infrastructure. These indicators were used to build a structured assessment of potential operational linkage.

However, blockchain investigations inherently contain attribution limitations. Wallet ownership cannot be definitively confirmed without additional off-chain evidence such as exchange records, KYC information, device attribution, subpoenaed platform data, or direct forensic access.

The findings presented in this report should therefore be considered an evidence-based analytical assessment derived from publicly available blockchain data and open-source intelligence techniques.

Certain wallet addresses, transaction identifiers, and sensitive references have been partially masked in this public portfolio version for privacy, professional, and responsible disclosure considerations.
